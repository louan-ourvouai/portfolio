About

I’m Louan Ourvouai, an engineering graduate from ENSIIE Paris (Évry-Courcouronnes) with four years of experience spanning Unity gameplay programming, C# / .NET software development, embedded systems, and blockchain/DeFi integration. I’m comfortable owning a project end to end: mobile and cross-platform delivery (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android), VR/XR development, and the full lifecycle from rapid prototyping to production in agile teams. I also build and ship my own projects, including Garypto, an automated crypto-trading bot, and Arduino-based electronics prototypes.
